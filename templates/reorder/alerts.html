📖 Reorder Point Explained
ROP = Avg × LT + Z × StdDev × √LT

For example: we sell 100 units/month, supplier takes 2 months. During lead time we expect to sell 100×2 = 200 units, so we reorder when stock hits 200. But 100 is only the average, so we add Safety Stock as a buffer against demand variability.

Safety Stock = Z × StdDev × √Lead Time
Z controls how conservative the buffer is; StdDev measures demand unpredictability; √LT scales the buffer to the duration of exposure. CV (Coefficient of Variation) = StdDev ÷ mean — expresses demand variability as a proportion of average demand (CV > 1 = highly lumpy/intermittent demand, treat with caution).
